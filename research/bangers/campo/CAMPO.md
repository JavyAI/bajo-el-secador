# En el campo (`campo` / `#campo`)

**Place:** Orocovis. The finca. Grandparents’ house.  
**Kicker:** Orocovis.  
**Not:** Colmado bocina. Galería porch JLG. Barbería típica amargue. Malecón couple-walk.

De día and de noche here are **time of day in the campo**, not 1980 vs 2011.  
Day is *Para Bailar La Bamba* timing: seis, plena, bomba, merengue típico, ranchera you can work to.  
Night is the radio after the dishes: trío, bolero, José José, El Puma.

PR jíbaro + DR Cibao sprinkled + Mexican records that actually played in that house.

---

## Door

| | #1 | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| De día | Ritchie Valens — *La Bamba* | The timing Javy named. Cuatro-speed dance. Official Topic. |
| De noche | Trío Vegabajeño — *En Mi Viejo San Juan* | The PR record the house knew. Topic. Javier’s VEVO cover sits after 15. *El Triste* in the 15. |

Forgotten in the 15, never opener: Cortijo/Ismael *El Negro Bembón*, Angel Viloria *Compadre Pedro Juan*, Los Panchos *Sin Ti*, Javier Solís *Sombras*.
