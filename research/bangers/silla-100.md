## Method

1. Build a culturally correct candidate pool: bachata típica 90s–00s first (Antony Santos, Luis Vargas, Raulín, Frank Reyes, Monchy y Alexandra, Yoskar, Luis Segura, Zacarías, Alex Bueno, Blas Durán + shop cousins), then Aventura-era / early Romeo Formula.
2. Attach official YouTube ids (VEVO, artist, JN Music Group, Planet, Karen, licensed Topic). Fan rips, IA “2.0”, mixes, lyrics channels dropped.
3. Sort that pool by official view count as a **resonance** signal (fetched 2026-08-16 via YouTube innertube `videoDetails.viewCount`).
4. Hand-check: would a Dominican man in the chair say **wow** in 15 seconds *in this room*?
5. Ban from core: Toby Love; Don Omar-led *Ella y Yo* as an opener; English/Spanglish R&B-bachata. One Royce max in discussion. 2022–23 urbano is HOY, not the spine.

## Limitations

- Views measure **YouTube upload resonance**, not RD radio spins or barbería play counts. 1990s típica masters often live on Topic with 1–10M views; Bronx VEVO has marketing, HD video, and a decade of autoplay.
- Some típica classics still lack a clean official HD single (*Take It Easy*, *La Chupadera*, several Blas Durán sides). Those stay culturally true and view-poor.
- Innertube `playabilityStatus` is `UNPLAYABLE` without `poToken`; that is not an embed signal. Ids were accepted on official channel + title match.
- A few shop cousins (Joe Veras, Héctor Acosta, Elvis Martínez, Teodoro, El Chaval) are not in the named 10-artist seed but are documented chair voices.
- Zacarías 2017 *Ya No Te Buscaré* (171M) is the same throat as the 2000s records and would fool a view sort into thinking the 2010s are the típica peak.
- Snapshot: 2026-08-16. Counts move.
