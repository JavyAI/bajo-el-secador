# Producer mindset — come to life

We produce **12 radio hours**, not a YouTube sort.  
Place first. Body first. The record you press play on has to work.

Read this before `GENRE-LOCK.md` / `CURATOR-LOOP.md` when a door is in doubt.

---

## The door test

Press play. Fifteen seconds. Does a Dominican **in this room** come to life?

| Pass | Fail |
|---|---|
| The body knows it before the lyric | Right song, dead clip |
| This place, this hour | Biggest Latin video on YouTube |
| Hips / throat / coin / mop / porch / hands — *this* room’s job | Neighbor’s job (Propuesta on Sunday, Suavemente in the dryer) |

Views pick the **official clip inside a locked pool**. Views do not pick #1.  
A dead clip also does not keep #1 just because the title is the room.

**Pasarela lesson (2026-08-23):** Los Hermanos Rosario — *Pasarela* is Sunday merengue. The official video is ~0.9M and does not explode. The door failed the body test. Forgotten #1 sits **in** the first 15, never as opener.

## The twelve hours

### 1. Colmado de día — the store speaker, yesterday

**Place:** sidewalk, crate, heat.  
**Body:** shout.  
**Hour:** salsa dura + merengue de fiesta. Lavoe, Celia, Gran Combo, Rebelión, Wilfrido, JLG merengue, Rosario.  
**Door job:** the 15-second bomb the corner already knows. *Suavemente* holds. *La Bilirrubina* / *El Africano* / *A Pedir Su Mano* live in the 15.  
**Broader hour:** still the store. Story-salsa (*Pedro Navaja*, *Gran Varón*) is catalog, not the first 15 seconds.

### 2. Colmado de noche — the store speaker, now

**Place:** same bocina, later hits.  
**Body:** shout.  
**Hour:** Yiyo, Chiquito, merengue still on the corner, 2010s salsa-radio late.  
**Door job:** Yiyo *Manos de Tijera* `ExCIp6TOnJw` (walked 2026-08-27). Official ~360M. *Abracadabra* was 7M — overtaken, sits in the 15. *La Llamada De Mi Ex* stays in this first 5 because the corner owns Chiquito.  
**Broader hour:** merengue + salsa that still plays outside. Not *Despacito*. Not dembow as the spine.

### 3. Secador de día — under the hood, yesterday

**Place:** women, rolos, alone with the song.  
**Body:** throat, not hips.  
**Hour:** balada, bolero, ranchera-pop weep, merengue sentimental (*Como Olvidar*, not *El Africano*).  
**Door job:** Selena *Como La Flor*. Accordion in, lump in the throat.  
**Broader hour:** JuanGa, Ana Gabriel, Camila, Sin Bandera, Chayanne weep. *Suave* is club, not the dryer.

### 4. Secador de noche — under the hood, now

**Place:** same chair, 2010s radio.  
**Body:** still throat.  
**Hour:** dryer-cry (Ha*Ash, Jesse & Joy, *Deja Vu*, Romeo violin a tía still plays).  
**Door job:** Royce & Shakira *Deja Vu*.  
**Broader hour:** the weep that still belts. Not *Pepas*. Not *Bella y Sensual*.

### 5. Barbería de día — the chair, yesterday

**Place:** men, diaspora shop, vellonera.  
**Body:** amargue. Coin.  
**Hour:** típica first (Antony, Vargas, Raulín, Segura, Frank, Mayimbe), then Aventura-era.  
**Door job:** *Obsesión*. Bronx after típica, not instead of it. *Voy Pa’llá* / *Pena* live in the 15.  
**Broader hour:** the machine, not the malecón. Toby Love is not the spine.

### 6. Barbería de noche — the chair, now

**Place:** same shop, Romeo-era.  
**Body:** amargue that still hurts.  
**Hour:** Romeo, Royce, Utopía, típica HOY (*La Asesina*, Héctor).  
**Door job:** *Propuesta Indecente*. That throne is this room’s. Other lists may keep the song after slot 5.  
**Broader hour:** bachata de noche, not Turizo chart-pop as the spine.

### 7. Limpieza de día — Sunday in the house, yesterday

**Place:** people who never leave. Mop, rice, the speaker in the sala.  
**Body:** merengue at volume. The house explodes.  
**Hour:** Milly, Wilfrido, Olga, JuanGa, Rosario, Eddy. Dryer balada at house volume is allowed; típica and salsa dura are not the spine.  
**Door job:** Toño *Kulikitaka*. Stupid-fun body hit. *Volvió Juanita* stays in the 15.  
**Broader hour:** clásicos del domingo. Quiet on TikTok and loud in the house is a pass.

### 8. Limpieza de noche — Sunday in the house, now

**Place:** same house, 2011+ radio.  
**Body:** merengue that starts the mop. People come to life.  
**Hour:** Toño, Olga, Rosario, Chiquito, JLG merengue, Eddy, Manny. After the merengue door, Sunday-house radio bombs that a Dominican actually mops to (*Vivir Mi Vida*, *La Bicicleta*, *Calma*) belong in the 15. Barbería bachata and dryer-cry are the tail, not the throne.  
**Door job:** Toño *Dale Vieja Dale* `hal7rXfJj5o`. Same family as *Kulikitaka*: yell, body, official clip that actually explodes (~78M).  
**Forgotten in the 15:** *Pasarela* — right Rosario title, dead official clip.  
**Not the door:** *La Llamada De Mi Ex* (Colmado de noche already owns that id in its first 5). *Propuesta* / Ha*Ash / *Robarte un Beso* (other rooms’ jobs).  
**Broader hour:** merengue first, then house radio. Do not hide billion-view Sunday songs behind 30 Manny Cruz album cuts.

### 9. Galería de día — the porch, yesterday

**Place:** sitting, not mopping, not dancing. Intergenerational.  
**Body:** the radio is already on. Conversation volume.  
**Hour:** JLG as house voice, Villalona, Sergio *Ventanita*, tía-radio boleros.  
**Door job:** *El Costo de la Vida*. *La Ventanita* / *Ojalá* live in the 15.

### 10. Galería de noche — the porch, now

### 11. Malecón de día — the couple, yesterday

**Place:** two people, anywhere (banco, car, waterfront).  
**Body:** hands. The walk.  
**Hour:** bachata romántica de pareja, bolero de dos, *Burbujas*, *Bachata Rosa*, Luismi de dos, Chayanne belt.

### 12. Malecón de noche — the couple, now

**Body:** hands, now.  
**Hour:** Romeo romantico, Royce, Vives/Yatra couple radio.

## How a producer orders a list

1. **#1** — door test. Javy walks it. Hygiene holds it.  
2. **First 15** — this room’s bangers. Wow in 15s. Forgotten #1 in the 15. Max two of the same lead. No consecutive lead. No other room’s first-5 id in *our* first 5.  
3. **16–100** — still the room. Views may order this block with artist spread. Neighbors with 15 minutes of fame live here, not on the throne.

Official only: VEVO / artist / label Topic / Fania / Warner / Karen / JN / Planet. No lyrics, karaoke, DJ mixes, AI, sped-up.

Vitilla and Marquesina stay off the board.
