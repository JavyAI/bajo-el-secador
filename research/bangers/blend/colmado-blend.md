## What you are hearing

The door titles are right. The **order** is a DJ that cuts merengue → salsa → merengue every other song. That is the jumpiness. A real colmado speaker does not ping-pong families. It holds a block, then crosses.

Spotify does not “blend BPM” the way a human DJ does. Radio / DJ / Smart Reorder style sequencing (the useful part for us) is:

1. **Stay in a family for 3–5 tracks.** Same dance, same tambora or same tumbao. The body settles.
2. **BPM proximity inside the family.** Merengue lives ~124–150. Salsa is felt ~88–110 (or ~170–220 clave). Those two numbers are not neighbors. A merengue-to-salsa cut is a floor drop even when both songs “peak.”
3. **One bridge, not a zipper.** Cross families once, with a shout that still works on the sidewalk (*Rebelión*, *Quimbara*, *Me Liberé*). Do not zipper M-S-M-S.
4. **Energy arc, not view rank.** Door = peak shout. Sauce 16–40 = still shout, still this room. Tail = story-salsa, outdoor romántica, neighbor guests, gated urbano.
5. **Key / Camelot is optional.** We do not have Spotify audio features on these YouTube ids. Family + BPM band is enough. Do not pretend we have a key for every clip.

We do **not** Billboard-sort. Views still only pick the official clip inside a locked pool.
