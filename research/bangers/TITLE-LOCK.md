# Title lock — wordmark + kicker

**Voice:** Balbuena naming the thing in the room. Luisito Martí’s neighbor, not a copywriter. Short. Concrete. Funny because it’s true.

**Three slots. Same shape on every page.**

| Slot | Job | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Kicker | The thing | `El` / `La` / `Los` + one noun |
| Linking | How you say it | `En el` / `En la` |
| Big word | The place | One hook word |

No *Domingo de*. No fragments that need a second sentence (*Ahí fue*, *Tú no ves*, *Se formó*).

| Nav | Kicker | Linking | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| Colmado | La bocina | En el | **colmado** |
| Secador | El chisme | En el | **secador** |
| Barbería | El corte | En la | **barbería** |
| Limpieza | El domingo | En la | **limpieza** |
| Galería | La radio | En la | **galería** |
| Malecón | Los novios | En el | **malecón** |

*El palo* = the banger / the house-lot heat. *La bocina* = the colmado speaker. *El chisme* = under the hood. *Los novios* = Balbuena teasing the couple.
