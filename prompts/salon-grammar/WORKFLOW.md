# Salón grammar — six rooms, one artist

Goal: every Ayer plate on En el secador is the same illustration as
saloon.wtf's Deluxe Saloon, translated to the Dominican Republic.
Grammar stays. Vocabulary swaps. Current time: mid-morning, 17 August 2026.

Source study: `style-guide.html` + `deluxe-saloon-source.webp`
Vocabulary: the locked Codex Ayer scenes we already have (location, cast, ritual).

## Goal

One imaginary Dominican illustrator. Six rooms. You can shuffle them
and still know they are siblings: faceless, flat, one beam, uniform grain,
Caribbean palette, unhurried Tuesday barrio — not a brochure.

## The eight laws (never swap)

1. Flat shapes, hard edges. No outlines. No gradients inside a shape.
2. Faceless figures. Identity = posture, clothes, hair, mustache, skin tone.
3. Stage-set camera. Frontal elevation, eye level, zero vanishing point.
4. One diagonal sunbeam, upper-left → lower-right. Only special effect.
5. Uniform grain over the whole image as a final pass.
6. Horizontal banding (road · sidewalk · shop · hillside · sky), adapted per room.
7. Off-center focal action, ~55% x, lower-middle.
8. Warm light, cool shade. Shadows are teal-black `#14211E`, never brown.

## Safe zones (16:9, 2560×1435)

- Title: upper-center quiet wall or sky. No heads, tools, roof edge, sun disk.
- Player: lower-center road / floor / promenade. No feet, chairs, animals.
- Clock: top-left calm. YT Music: top-right calm.

## Workflow

1. Lock grammar (this file).
2. Generate **barbería** first — closest sibling to Deluxe Saloon. Style lock.
3. Review against the eight laws. Reject if any law breaks.
4. Generate the other five from the same prompt skeleton, only the room block changes.
5. Install keepers at 2560×1435 (or resample 16:9). Do not re-crop.
6. Presente later: same plates, clothes only. Do not restyle again.

## Reject

Photoreal, oil, gouache, outlines, faces with eyes/mouths, neon, resort props,
poverty theater, painted titles, gadget piles, second artist.
