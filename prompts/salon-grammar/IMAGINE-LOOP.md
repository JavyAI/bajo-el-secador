Seed style from `deluxe-saloon-source.png` only. Never seed from oil or a failed plate.

## Pass / fail (any one fail = redo)

1. Flat hard-edged blocks. No oil. No photoreal. No airbrush.
2. Faceless Muñeca heads. Hair is the portrait. No eyes, no mouths.
3. Stage-set camera. No vanishing point.
4. One cream beam, upper-left → lower-right.
5. Uniform grain over the whole frame.
6. Horizontal bands. Shop/house ~60% height.
7. Focal ~55% x, lower-middle.
8. Dominican people + Bidó Caribbean color.
9. This room’s ritual is readable.
10. No painted words.

## Loop

1. Critique the live plate against the ten laws.
2. If fail: `image_edit` Deluxe Saloon + the room script. Full STYLE paragraph.
3. Critique the result. Keep or retry once.
4. Presente is clothes/street only, seeded from the Ayer keeper.
5. Install only keepers.
