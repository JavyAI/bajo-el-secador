A Dominican open-bay barbershop as a finished contemporary oil painting: one enormous coral-peach facade with teal trim and a rusted zinc roof, a wide dark interior bay like a stage. First read is a barber in a teal shirt cutting a caped client in a red hydraulic chair, both isolated by a cream sunbeam entering the bay. Two waiting men sit in cool shade inside; a woman in mango and purple walks the sidewalk; a striped pole at the right corner. Bougainvillea, stacked mint-and-rosa houses, cobalt sky. Loaded visible brushstrokes, cracked sun-baked plaster, thick pigment, painterly Dominican faces. Wide 16:9 cinematic plate, quiet sky above, quiet asphalt below. No lettering, logos, phones, or signatures.
