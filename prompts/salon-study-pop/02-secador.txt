A Dominican neighborhood beauty salon interior as a finished contemporary oil painting: two-tone turquoise walls, patterned tile, one cream sunbeam pouring through an open street door at left. First read is a stylist in a grey tee blow-drying a seated woman's hair with a round brush, that pass isolated inside the beam. An abuela in pink rollers combs a little girl's curls on a floral bench; another woman sits under a hood dryer. Mute unlabeled bottles, folded towels, one small analog radio on a wooden shelf, a round wall fan. Loaded visible brushstrokes, thick pigment, painterly Dominican faces, hard sun into cool interior shade. Wide 16:9 cinematic plate, quiet wall above, quiet tile below. No lettering, logos, phones, or signatures.
