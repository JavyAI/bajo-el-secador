A Dominican house gallery as a finished contemporary oil painting: one enormous turquoise clapboard wall, coral dado, cobalt-blue shutters, a cream sunbeam lying across a small wooden table. First read is an elder in a yellow shirt tuning a single portable radio while a younger woman leans in to listen, both inside the beam. Two more family members sit close on wooden chairs. Bougainvillea as a magenta mass at left, deep porch shade, weathered painted wood. Loaded visible brushstrokes, thick pigment, painterly Dominican faces. Wide 16:9 cinematic plate, quiet wall above, quiet porch floor below. One radio only. No lettering, logos, phones, or signatures.
