A Dominican corner grocery as a finished contemporary oil painting: one enormous two-tone wall of wet sea-turquoise over ripe mango, paint peeling to grey block, a rusted zinc awning over an open service window. First read is a clerk in a white shirt passing a paper parcel to a neighbor in a cobalt cap, both caught inside one cream sunbeam slicing from the upper left. Three men play dominó at a small table in deep teal shade; a mother and child wait by a mass of bougainvillea. Hard Caribbean noon, loaded visible brushstrokes, thick pigment, painterly Dominican faces, weathered plaster. Wide 16:9 cinematic plate, quiet cobalt sky above stacked barrio roofs, quiet cracked asphalt below. No lettering, logos, phones, or signatures.
