A Dominican barrio porch on cleaning day as a finished contemporary oil painting: one enormous teal house with a marigold lintel, wet coral floor tiles flashing inside one cream sunbeam. First read is an adult woman mopping the porch while a little girl holds a yellow cloth in the doorway and another adult stands with a yellow bucket. Receding pastel street, water tanks, a mango tree, a mass of bougainvillea. Loaded visible brushstrokes, peeled plaster, thick pigment, painterly Dominican faces, hard Caribbean sun. Wide 16:9 cinematic plate, quiet sky above, quiet wet street below. Child stays with the adults. No lettering, logos, phones, or signatures.
